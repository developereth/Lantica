About Lantica 1960

Since 1996

Lantica 1960 has been serving authentic Ethiopian cuisine with modern elegance. Located in the heart of Arat Kilo, Addis Ababa, we bring traditional flavors to your table with love and authenticity.

Our chefs use family recipes passed down through generations, ensuring every dish tells a story of Ethiopian heritage. From our signature Yefisk Agelgel to the traditional Coffee Ceremony, every experience at Lantica is crafted with care.

Our Promise

To provide an unforgettable dining experience where tradition meets innovation, and every guest leaves with a full heart and happy taste buds.
