About Lantica 1960

Since 1996

Lantica 1960 has been serving authentic Ethiopian cuisine with modern elegance. Located in the heart of Arat Kilo, Addis Ababa, we bring traditional flavors to your table.

Our chefs use family recipes passed down through generations, ensuring every dish tells a story of Ethiopian heritage.

Our Mission

To provide an unforgettable dining experience where tradition meets innovation, and every guest feels like family.
